# Layers 3 and 4: transactions and series

Concrete fact patterns, with each step of the analysis proven as a theorem.

- `SpouseRepurchase.lean`: shares sold at a loss and bought by the spouse five days later
- `SiblingAndLateRepurchase.lean`: a sibling buys in the period and the taxpayer buys back on day 31
- `TfsaContribution.lean`: shares contributed in kind to a TFSA at a loss
- `HoldcoRepurchase.lean`: shares bought back by a holding company the spouses control
- `SisterCompanyRepurchase.lean`: a company's loss suspended when its sister company buys the shares
- `Crystallization.lean`: the capital gains exemption crystallized under 85(1), then a surplus strip caught by 84.1
- `SaleThroughHoldco.lean`: a capital dividend and a pre-sale dividend caught by 55(2), then a capital dividend out of Holdco
- `PostMortemPipeline.lean`: the gain taxed at death, and the estate's sale to its own corporation, with no dividend under 84.1
- `EstateFreeze.lean`: a freeze under 86 in favour of minor children, and corporate attribution under 74.4(2)
- `DonatingShares.lean`: donating shares in kind or selling them first, with federal tax computed for the year
- `InvestmentIncomeIntegration.lean`: interest earned personally or through a holding company, federal tax on each route
- `EligibleDesignation.lean`: how much of a dividend to designate eligible, against the general rate income pool and Part III.1 tax
- `CrystallizationMinimumTax.lean`: the minimum tax on a gain fully sheltered by the capital gains deduction
- `DebtSwap.lean`: selling a portfolio to repay a mortgage and borrowing to buy it back, under 20(1)(c)
- `AttendantCare.lean`: attendant care claimed as a medical expense, or $10,000 of it with the disability credit
- `PensionSplit.lean`: how much pension income spouses should split
- `SalaryOrDividend.lean`: paying the owner-manager a salary or dividends, with CPP
- `SupportArrears.lean`: support paid short and caught up, with child support first
